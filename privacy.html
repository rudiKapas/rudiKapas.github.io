Privacy policy

How the Ubuntu Drive Sync personal-use OAuth application handles Google account access, file data and authentication credentials.

What information is accessed

When the owner authorizes the application, Rclone may access Google Drive files and metadata as permitted by the approved OAuth scope. The configured synchronization job uses this access to read and update files in folders explicitly chosen by the owner. The OAuth scope itself may authorize access to files outside those selected folders.

How information is used

Google Drive file data is used solely to synchronize files between the owner's local computer and the owner's authorized Google Drive account. The site does not request access to or collect Google Drive contents. No Google Drive data is sold or used for advertising, analytics, or model training by this personal-use application.

Storage, sharing and retention

File copies are retained in the owner's selected local folders and in Google Drive according to the owner's synchronization settings. OAuth credentials are held in the local Rclone configuration on the owner's computer, not on this GitHub Pages website. File transfers occur between the local computer and Google Drive APIs; this static website does not operate a file storage server. Google and GitHub independently process data needed to operate their respective services under their own policies.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can stop scheduled synchronization, delete local Rclone credentials and revoke the application's Google account access at Google Account connections. The owner can delete local or Google Drive copies using the usual file management controls. As with any two-way synchronization tool, deletions may propagate unless synchronization is stopped first.

Contact and changes

This application is maintained for private use. Questions or requests about these pages may be submitted through the website repository's issues. This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the local setup.
