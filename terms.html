Terms of use

Usage conditions for the Ubuntu Drive Sync personal-use OAuth application.

Purpose and access

Ubuntu Drive Sync is a privately operated configuration using Rclone to synchronize selected files between the owner's Ubuntu computer and a Google Drive account. This website is informational only and does not provide cloud storage or offer a public synchronization service.

Authorization and responsibilities

The owner must authorize access to an account they are permitted to use and comply with applicable Google terms and any organizational rules. The owner decides which local and cloud folders to synchronize, maintains appropriate backups, and protects OAuth credentials stored on their device.

Sync and risk

Two-way synchronization may copy changes and propagate deletions. Rclone is separate open-source software supplied under its own terms. This personal-use setup is provided without any guarantee of uninterrupted availability or data preservation. It does not override policies governing an organizational Google account.

Questions

See the privacy policy or contact the maintainer via GitHub issues.
